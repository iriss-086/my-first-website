Our story

We make pet adoption feel warm, informed, and stress-free from the first search to the ride home.

🐾
Better matches, happier homes
Started with one shelter visit

Helping people meet pets they are truly ready to love

Pawfect Match began in 2024 after our founder, Maya Chen, spent a Saturday helping a neighborhood shelter update adoption profiles. She noticed that wonderful pets were being overlooked because families did not know which companions would fit their routines, homes, and comfort levels.

Today we work with local shelters, foster groups, trainers, and veterinary partners to turn adoption into a guided experience. We listen first, match thoughtfully, and stay close after adoption so every new family has practical support when it matters most.

What we believe

Our work is built on patience, good information, and care for both sides of every adoption.

🏡

Every pet deserves a home

Shy seniors, bouncy puppies, calm cats, and curious rabbits all deserve a safe place where they can be understood.

💛

Adoption, not shopping

We champion rescue-first choices and help adopters see the joy, personality, and potential already waiting in shelters.

🤝

Support for life

Great matches still need guidance, so we offer check-ins, behavior resources, and referrals after the adoption day photos.

🌆

Community first

Our strongest outcomes come from local shelter teams, fosters, volunteers, and neighbors solving problems together.
