Frequently asked questions

Adoption fees usually range from $75 to $275 depending on the pet's species, age, and medical needs. Each adoption includes a wellness exam, age-appropriate vaccines, microchip, spay or neuter surgery when applicable, parasite treatment, and 30 days of post-adoption support from our team.
Start by browsing available pets or taking the match quiz. Once you find a pet who feels like a good fit, submit an inquiry and we will schedule a conversation, answer care questions, and arrange a meet-and-greet. If everyone feels comfortable, we complete the adoption agreement and send you home with records, transition tips, and a starter care plan.
Adopters must be at least 18 years old and able to provide a safe, stable home. Renters should confirm that pets are allowed before applying. We do not require a fenced yard for every dog, but we do match each pet to a home that fits their exercise level, temperament, and comfort around children or other animals.
If the match is not working within the first 14 days, contact us right away. We will help troubleshoot common transition issues, and if returning the pet is the kindest option, we will welcome them back without judgment. The adoption fee can be transferred to another Pawfect Match adoption within six months.
Fosters provide a temporary home while a pet waits for adoption, recovers from medical care, or needs a quieter place to decompress. Pawfect Match supplies food, basic gear, veterinary care, and coaching. You can choose short-term, weekend, or longer foster placements based on your schedule and experience.
