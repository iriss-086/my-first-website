How it works

Adopting is easy

We keep the process clear, caring, and low-pressure from your first quiz answer to the ride home.

1
📝

Take the 60-second quiz

Share your living space, schedule, experience level, and energy preferences so we can narrow the search.

2
🤝

Meet your matched pets

Book friendly introductions with pets whose temperament and care needs line up with your day-to-day life.

3
🏠

Welcome them home

Our adoption team helps with paperwork, transition tips, and follow-up support after the first tail wag.

Why adopt with us

Good matches start with good care

Every adoption is supported by shelter partners, medical checks, and humans who know each pet well.

🩺

Vet-checked & vaccinated

Each pet receives a wellness exam, core vaccines, and clear medical notes before meeting adopters.

💞

14-day love-it guarantee

If the fit is not right, we guide a kind transition and keep working with you on a better match.

📞

Lifetime adopter support

Training tips, nutrition questions, and behavior check-ins stay available long after adoption day.

🌱

100% nonprofit

Adoption fees go back into rescue transport, foster supplies, medical care, and shelter grants.
